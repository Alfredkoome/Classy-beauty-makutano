About Us

Where Beauty Meets Artistry

Classy Beauty Haven Makutano was born from a simple passion: making every client feel radiant, confident and cared for. Nestled in the heart of Makutano, we've grown into a trusted name for nail care, grooming and beauty treatments — built one satisfied client at a time.

Every visit begins with meticulous hygiene standards and ends with a result you'll love. Our experienced beauticians use premium products and refined techniques to deliver treatments that are as safe as they are stunning. From your first appointment to your hundredth, our promise never changes — perfection, every time.
